Heading Level 2: Overview

The Buffalo National River flows freely for about 135 miles through northern Arkansas. It was the first national river in the United States.

Heading Level 3: Activities

Visitors enjoy canoeing, hiking, fishing, and camping along the river.

Heading Level 4: Seasons

Spring is the best time for floating, when water levels are highest.

Heading Level 5: Safety

Always check water conditions and wear a life jacket.

Lists

Ordered List: Planning a Float Trip

  1. Check the river level.
  2. Reserve a canoe.
  3. Pack water and snacks.
  4. Launch at the access point.

Unordered List: What to Bring

  • Sunscreen
  • Life jacket
  • Dry bag
  • Camera

Definition List: River Terms

Bluff
A steep cliff rising above the river.
Gravel bar
A deposit of gravel along the river bank, good for stopping to rest.
Put-in
The place where you launch your boat.

Quotes

As the saying goes, the river is the journey, and that rings true here.

The Buffalo National River was established in 1972 to preserve one of the few remaining unpolluted, free-flowing rivers in the lower 48 states.
